01 / The identity

More than
a name.

FLOWERS LAND is an independent clothing brand exploring the space between street culture, graphic expression, and everyday uniform.

We believe clothing can carry an idea. Every detail — from the silhouette to the mark, from the artwork to the label — has a reason to exist.

FWLD is not built to follow every direction. It is built to develop its own language, one chapter at a time.

Founded by Dika Fabrizio Nugroho · 23 October 2026
