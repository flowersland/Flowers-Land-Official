01 / THE ORIGIN

Not made to follow. Made to express.

FLOWERS LAND is an independent streetwear brand from Indonesia, built around the idea that individuality should never be limited by expectations.

We explore the relationship between design, culture and personal expression through clothing and visual identity.

Every detail is part of a larger language. A language that allows people to express who they are, in their own way.
